ABOUT INSTRIDE

Practitioner-led. Built from real operational experience.

InStride LLC was created to provide practical professional development grounded in real-world leadership, manufacturing, operations, continuous improvement, engineering, maintenance and reliability, plant startups, and structured problem solving.

The goal is simple: participants should leave with more than notes from a presentation. They should leave with tools, language, and approaches they can apply immediately.

“The objective is not to deliver another class. It is to help people lead better, think better, and solve the problems that are getting in the way of the work.”
