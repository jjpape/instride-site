ABOUT INSTRIDE

Practitioner-led. Built from real operational experience.

InStride was founded by Jereb Pape to provide practical professional development grounded in the realities leaders face every day.

About Jereb Pape

Jereb Pape is an experienced leader, educator, and problem solver with more than 18 years of experience across operations, manufacturing, continuous improvement, engineering, maintenance and reliability, supply chain, organizational leadership, and workforce development.

His career has spanned Fortune 500 and private-equity-backed organizations, from plant operations with approximately 20 team members to complex sites of more than 750 employees. His experience also extends beyond individual facilities, working with external supply chain and co-manufacturing partners throughout the United States and Canada.

His background includes plant leadership, operational excellence, plant startups, equipment reliability, analytics, leader development, employee engagement, and cross-functional problem solving. He has led the startup of a USDA-regulated manufacturing facility and helped organizations improve safety, quality, productivity, downtime, yield, standard work, TPM, and decision-making.

Before beginning his civilian career, Jereb served four years in the United States Coast Guard, supporting search and rescue and port security missions.

Jereb has experience in labor relations training and has worked in both union and non-union plant environments, providing practical perspective on leadership, communication, employee relations, and organizational dynamics.

He has also taught MBA-level courses, including Methods of Quality Management and Global Methods of Quality Management, combining business concepts with practical experience in operations, leadership, quality, and continuous improvement.

Jereb holds an MBA and a Master of Strategic Leadership from Mount Mercy University, along with a BBA from Strayer University. Through InStride, he brings together operational experience, business thinking, leadership development, education, and structured problem solving to help people and organizations perform at a higher level.

“The objective isn't to deliver another class. It's to help people think better, lead better, and solve the problems getting in the way of the work.”
